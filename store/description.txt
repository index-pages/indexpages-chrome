Index Pages speeds up the indexing of your URLs in search engines. This extension is a companion to your Index Pages account: collect pages while you browse and send them as an order without leaving the tab.

What it does

• Add this page – one click puts the current tab's address into a list kept in your browser.
• Right-click any link and choose "Add link to Index Pages list", or right-click a page and choose "Add this page to Index Pages list". The toolbar icon shows how many URLs are waiting.
• Paste more URLs, one per line. Duplicates, non-web addresses and local or private addresses are filtered out before anything is sent.
• Create draft – turns the list into a draft order in your account. Drafts cost nothing.
• Create and run – creates the order, then shows exactly what it will cost against your balance and runs it only after you confirm.
• See your balance, the price per URL and your ten most recent orders with their status and progress. Every order links to its page in the dashboard.
• Errors from the service are shown in plain words, including which URL was rejected and why. If the balance is too low, a link takes you to the top-up page.

Your key, your control

The extension works with an API key you create in the Index Pages dashboard (Settings → API keys). Each key carries scopes, and the extension only offers what the key allows. If you want drafts only, create a key without the orders:run scope: such a key cannot spend money at all. A double click or a lost connection never creates two orders or charges twice.

Privacy

The key is stored only in this browser's local extension storage and is not synced. The extension contacts one server, api.index-pages.com, and sends it only the URLs you choose and your key. It reads a page's address only when you click "Add this page" or use the right-click menu. No analytics, no tracking, no other data collected.

You need an Index Pages account with a verified email to create a key. Orders are paid from your prepaid balance at the price shown in the dashboard.
